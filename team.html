Operators first.

Auxerion is built by people who have run the kind of work it makes software for. Engineering that holds up under load, creative direction for products used at scale, operations across very different industries, and the commercial work of getting them adopted.

The executive team.

A small group, deliberately. Each has spent a career in environments where handoffs fail loudly, where the work continues long after launch, and where a tool that adds steps gets abandoned. That is the problem Auxerion exists to solve.

Don GoddardChief Technology Officer

Don has spent more than thirty years building the layer other software runs on. He has designed and engineered over a dozen engines and platforms across console, desktop and mobile, and has been a technical founder or C-level leader at several companies, including ones that reached an exit. Much of his simulation work has been for military, medical and educational programs, where a system has to behave predictably and account for what it did. He has run engineering groups of more than a hundred people and two-person founding teams, and carried products from first idea through production to launch.

Terrence HicksCreative Director

Terrence decides how a product looks and how it feels to use, and has done it for more than two decades across desktop, mobile and web. His range covers the whole creative lifecycle: early exploration, the production pipelines that keep a large team moving, and the long stretch of running a product once people depend on it every day. He has led multi-disciplinary teams of more than a hundred across several countries, and co-founded a studio of his own. He works best where design, engineering and user experience all have to agree.

Matthew VevesOperations Director

Matt works on how organizations actually run. He spent over a decade as an associate partner at a global management consultancy, and has held operating roles across software, interactive entertainment and heavy industry, covering operations, finance, research and analytics. That range is the point. Auxerion builds for three industries at once, so the work of translating between them, and of keeping the company's own operations coordinated, sits with him.

Klas Charles-HarrisExecutive Vice President, Sales
