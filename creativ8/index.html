The design exists everywhere — and nowhere.

Docs, boards, chats, tasks, and files each hold a piece. The GDD is obsolete before it is finished. Design mistakes surface when they cost the most. And when people leave, the project's memory leaves with them.

One living project graph connects everything.

Every character, system, location, document, task, and asset is a Smart Object. Change one and every live view updates. Commitments that depend on it — issued briefs, approved tasks, engine contracts — are flagged for review, never silently rewritten. Every important decision keeps its why.

Creativ8 investor overview

Game development is a large, global, creative industry that still runs its most important operational work on general-purpose documents and spreadsheets. Creativ8 targets the studio's core loop — design to production — with an AI-native product that has no direct modern equivalent. The current build is a deep functional prototype: eight modules, twenty-six tools, one shared object graph, contract-tested end to end. The beachhead is mid-size and independent studios, where the pain is sharpest and adoption decisions are fast; the plan is early-access studio pilots, then broader testing, then public launch.
