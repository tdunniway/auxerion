Kept investor overview

Independent full-service hotels sit between enterprise platforms built for chains and point solutions that each own one slice, and none of them know whether the hotel kept its promise. Kept targets the operational layer above the PMS with a promise-centric model, purpose-built role experiences, graduated autonomy, and a manual-first path that opens properties without sophisticated systems, including in markets competitors serve poorly. The investor demonstration shows guided arrival, experience-recovery, and room-service journeys end to end on a synthetic property with private server records and outcome receipts; a pilot property is the next milestone. Market sizing, pricing, and pilot evidence are supplied in the investor overview.
