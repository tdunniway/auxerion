One company. Three industries. One idea.

Auxerion builds AI-native operating systems for industries where complex, fast-moving teams still run on fragmented software.

We chose game development, medical practice operations, and hotel operations deliberately.

They share the traits that make operational software hard and valuable: complex multi-step work, real-time pressure, skilled but non-technical teams, fragmented incumbents, and no modern operating layer.
