One loop.
Three industries.

A signal becomes owned work. AI prepares the next step. A named person approves anything consequential. Evidence closes it, and the outcome is verified rather than assumed. Each of our products arrived at this loop on its own, in its own language. That is why one company can build deeply for three industries — and why the next vertical is faster than the last.
